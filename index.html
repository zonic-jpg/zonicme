About Us

ZonicMe is the parent group behind a family of Nigerian and pan-African consumer apps. We build products across fashion, art, media and everyday planning — each solving a real need for the continent and its diaspora.

Our mission is to empower Africans through the creative economy: creators earn, audiences discover, and brands connect with real communities across the orbit.

What the orbit reveals

One family of apps — clear value for people who use them, and clear opportunity for brands who meet them there.

For users

  • Discover African fashion, art, media and celebrations in one trusted orbit
  • Tools for style, planning and events that fit everyday life across Africa and the diaspora
  • Earn rewarded attention on AdSpot while keeping control of what you engage with
  • Move between apps without losing the ZonicMe parent you already trust

For brands

  • Reach consented audiences inside fashion, art, media and celebration contexts
  • Partner across the orbit instead of rebuilding presence app by app
  • Use privacy-first, anonymised orbit signals to understand demand — not personal dossiers
  • Activate campaigns, sponsorships and marketplace presence where culture already lives
