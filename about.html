About Us

One parent group, five apps, a single mission across Africa and the diaspora.

ZonicMe is the parent group behind a family of Nigerian and pan-African consumer apps. We build products across fashion, art, media and everyday planning — each solving a real need for the continent and its diaspora.

Our mission is to empower Africans through the creative economy: creators earn, audiences discover, and brands connect with real communities across the orbit.

What the orbit reveals

One family of apps — clear value for people who use them, and clear opportunity for brands who meet them there.

  • Discover African fashion, art, media and celebrations in one trusted orbit

For brands
